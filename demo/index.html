02 / Research context

Embodied AI for
Scientific User Facilities

We study learned robotic manipulation of fragile scientific specimens at DOE user facilities, beginning at SLAC’s MeV-UED facility. This site shares an early tool-use simulation study based on SimToolReal and its DexToolBench tasks.

Follow the project on GitHub ↗

What counts as completion?

Every waypoint must be reached in order. All four corresponding tool keypoints must be within 1.5 cm for one control step, so both position and orientation matter. The threshold uses the released evaluator’s nominal 1 cm tolerance and 1.5 keypoint scale.

These are rigid-tool pose-tracking simulations. Bristle flex, ink deposition, dirt removal, and nail insertion are not simulated. The clips are not real-robot demonstrations or beamline digital twins.

Recorded poses are replayed at 15 frames per second, with a one-second final hold in videos. No policy inference or server-side simulation is needed to view them.
