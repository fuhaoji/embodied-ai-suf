Recorded simulation, played in your browser. This is a scripted full-state/IK demonstration. No live policy inference, simulation server, or dedicated GPU is needed to view it.

The four claws capture each screw beneath its head without magnetic attachment. The hand places and releases all eleven screws, parks the grabber, and picks up a powered screwdriver to rotate them. Ideal seated retention approximates thread engagement; detailed threads, bending, tightening torque, and preload are not modeled.

Two validated recordings are shown with a marked scene cut: a free-cover placement in Isaac Lab / Newton / MuJoCo-Warp, followed by screw installation in native MuJoCo using the audited Isaac Lab scene. The second recording begins with the cover seated and fixed; the handoff between the recordings was not simulated.
